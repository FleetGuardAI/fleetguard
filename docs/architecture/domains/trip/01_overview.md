# Trip Management Domain: Overview

## Purpose
The Trip Management Domain acts as the authoritative source of truth for the physical movement of vehicles across the FleetGuard platform. A Trip represents a business fact: the start, continuous movement, and eventual cessation of a vehicle's motion. 

## Why the Domain Exists
In a logistics environment, raw GPS telemetry is too granular and noisy for high-level business intelligence. Conversely, a `Dispatch` represents the operational *plan* to move, but not the physical reality. The Trip Domain bridges this gap by distilling continuous data (ignition events, position tracking) into discrete, highly consistent business entities (Trips) that the rest of the enterprise can consume to measure performance, driver behavior, and asset utilization.

## Responsibilities
- **Lifecycle Tracking:** Establishing the start, pause, resume, and completion of vehicle movement.
- **Route Metrics:** Deriving aggregate facts like `total_distance`, `driving_duration`, `idle_duration`, `origin`, and `destination`.
- **Event Orchestration:** Reacting to `OperationalEvents` and emitting specialized `DomainEvents` for intelligence consumption.

## Non-Responsibilities
- **Raw Telemetry Storage:** Trip does not ingest raw GPS JSON packets. It acts strictly on parsed Operational Events.
- **Human Assignments:** Trip does not determine who is driving. It merely references the `driver_assignment_id` provided by the Driver Assignment domain.
- **Dispatching:** A trip is not a plan; it is a fact. It has no concept of "ETA", "Scheduled Route", or "Late Departure".
- **Hardware Management:** Trip does not care about tracker health or fuel sensors.

## Relationship with FleetGuard
The Trip domain operates autonomously at the center of the Event-Driven Architecture.
- It consumes `OperationalEvents` generated by the **Device Registry** and **Gateway**.
- It queries the **Driver Assignment** domain to attach temporal driver contexts to trips.
- It produces `DomainEvents` consumed by the **Fleet Intelligence Engine** for evaluating risk and anomalous behavior.

## Bounded Context
The Trip Bounded Context is strictly confined to the chronological and spatial bounding of a single movement cycle. It is isolated from Identity (Vehicle, Driver) and external Planning (Dispatch), coupled to them solely via universally unique identifiers (UUIDs).
